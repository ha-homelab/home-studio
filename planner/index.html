FROM BLANK PAGE TO HOME

Draw your first home.

New to floorplans? Follow the illustrated two-room tutorial. Each step shows what to click and what you should see. This website works in your browser; no Python or installation is needed.

  1. Draw the rooms.

    Drag a rectangle on the metric plan, or add a room by dimensions in Floor settings. Edit its polygon points for other shapes. Rooms define floor areas only.

  2. Give them walls.

    Select a room and choose Build wall outline, or draw individual wall segments. Exact shared walls are reused. Walls stay separate when rooms move.

  3. Open up the space.

    Choose Add door or Add window, then click a wall. Adjust width, distance from wall start, sill, and head in the inspector. These are actual holes in the 3D walls.

  4. Make it your own.

    Open the Item library, choose a design, and click inside a room to place it. Change its finish and color in Item details, inspect the 3D preview, and export JSON when ready. Entity IDs are optional; leave them blank for a drawing.

FROM DRAWING TO HOME ASSISTANT

Export, build, and install your plan.

The complete public toolkit includes the compiler and Home Assistant installer. Export your drawing here, then run the local tools on your computer with Python 3.10 or newer. This browser editor does not connect to or control Home Assistant.

  1. Save a portable draft.

    Download project.json and keep it somewhere you can find again. This file contains your drawing and any entity IDs you entered. It is not an installed dashboard.

  2. Get the complete toolkit.

    Download and extract the public package. The local compiler and installer use Python's standard library; no extra packages are needed.

    Download the complete ZIP ↗
  3. Build, preview, and install.

    The guide walks through the local build, your Home Assistant config folder, a dry run, backups, the YAML entry, and restart. It includes a file-copy route for Home Assistant OS.

    Complete installation guide ↗
Build command for the downloaded toolkit

Open a terminal in the extracted toolkit folder. Replace /path/to/project.json with the actual location of your exported file; quote paths containing spaces.

python3 scripts/build-planner-project.py --project /path/to/project.json --output dist/my-home --ha-package

This creates your viewer and dist/my-home/home-floorplan.tar.gz. Follow the guide to preview and install it. Keep your editable JSON outside the generated output folder.
