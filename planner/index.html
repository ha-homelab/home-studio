FROM BLANK PAGE TO HOME

Draw your first home.

New to floorplans? Follow the illustrated two-room tutorial. Each step shows what to click and what you should see. Follow the illustrated two-room tutorial. This website works in your browser; no Python or installation is needed.

  1. Draw the rooms.

    Drag a rectangle on the metric plan, or add a room by dimensions in Floor settings. Edit its polygon points for other shapes. Rooms define floor areas only.

  2. Give them walls.

    Select a room and choose Build wall outline, or draw individual wall segments. Exact shared walls are reused. Walls stay separate when rooms move.

  3. Open up the space.

    Choose Add door or Add window, then click a wall. Adjust width, distance from wall start, sill, and head in the inspector. These are actual holes in the 3D walls.

  4. Make it your own.

    Open the Item library, choose a design, and click inside a room to place it. Change its finish and color in Item details, inspect the 3D preview, and export JSON when ready. Entity IDs are optional; leave them blank for a drawing.

YOUR FIRST HOME IN HOME ASSISTANT

From one room to 3D Home

  1. Draw a room

    Choose Start with measurements, name your room, and enter its width and depth in meters. Keep Include walls checked to create the room and its walls together.

  2. Add furniture, doors, and windows

    Open the Item library, choose a design, then click inside your room to place it. Choose Add door or Add window, then click a wall. Select an item to change its size and appearance in Item details.

  3. Add devices, if you want

    Select your room and choose its Home Assistant area. Under Add device to this room, choose a device and select Add linked item, then drag it into place. To connect an item you already placed, select it and find Home Assistant in Item details. You can also leave the drawing unlinked.

  4. Save and publish

    Check Preview 3D. Above the editor, choose Save draft, then Publish to 3D Home. Open 3D Home to see the published plan and use its device controls. Saving a draft keeps your work in Home Assistant; the published plan changes only when you publish.

Drag an object to move it; hold Alt to bypass the 0.25 m snap. Resize a new room with its corner handles or Width and Depth fields; an exclusive wall outline and its openings follow. In Preview 3D, choose Edit items to drag furniture and use its ↕ Y handle to change height. The y field and Raise / Lower buttons also set height. Escape cancels a drag; Undo reverses a completed change. Original generated room shapes stay protected.

Drawing and publishing do not operate devices. Use More options → Export drawing (JSON) above the editor for a portable backup.

FROM DRAWING TO HOME ASSISTANT

Export, build, and install your plan.

The complete public toolkit includes the compiler and Home Assistant installer. Export your drawing here, then run the local tools on your computer with Python 3.10 or newer. This browser editor does not connect to or control Home Assistant.

Prefer drawing directly in Home Assistant? Set up Home Studio to link your areas and devices, save drafts, and publish from its sidebar. The browser editor remains available for offline work and JSON exchange.

  1. Save a portable draft.

    Download project.json and keep it somewhere you can find again. This file contains your drawing and any entity IDs you entered. It is not an installed dashboard.

  2. Get the complete toolkit.

    Download and extract the public package. The local compiler and installer use Python's standard library; no extra packages are needed.

Build command for the downloaded toolkit

Open a terminal in the extracted toolkit folder. Replace the project path with the location of your exported file; quote paths containing spaces.

python3 scripts/build-planner-project.py --project /path/to/project.json --output dist/my-home --ha-package

This creates your viewer and installation archive. Follow the guide to preview and install it. Keep your editable JSON outside the generated output folder.
