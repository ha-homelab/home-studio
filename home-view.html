      Check position after power loss
      
A connected motor can still have an inaccurate travel reference. HA may repeat an old percentage after reconnecting.

      
  1. When the room can safely be fully opened or closed, choose Open or Close above and watch the curtain reach the endpoint. Stop remains available.
  2. Check the other endpoint, then at least two partial positions using Set position. Compare the real curtain with the HA-reported percentage; small endpoint differences are kept as reported.
  3. If a partial target fails, leave power on and try a deliberate full endpoint movement before checking again.

      
No movement starts by opening this guide or recording a check.
