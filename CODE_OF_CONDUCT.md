# Code of Conduct

Everyone participating in this project is expected to be respectful, considerate
and constructive, regardless of background, identity or experience. Discuss
technical evidence, welcome questions, acknowledge mistakes and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
trolling and publication of another person's private information are not
acceptable. These expectations apply to project repositories, issues, pull
requests and official project communications.

Report concerns privately to **alvit.work@gmail.com**. Include links and context
without publishing sensitive details. The primary maintainer reviews reports
confidentially as far as practical, considers the parties' accounts and may
remove content, issue a warning or restrict participation proportionately.
Retaliation is prohibited. A reporter or affected participant can ask for
reconsideration through the same private contact with additional evidence.

There is currently no independent conduct moderator. A complaint involving the
maintainer cannot be promised independent review; GitHub's platform reporting
facilities remain available for violations of its policies.
